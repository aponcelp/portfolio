# Strategy and operating design

**2025 – 2026 · Executive team, CX and GTM leadership**

The other half of my job was designing how the CX organisation runs, and writing the case for it. I wrote these as short decision documents: the proposed answer, the case against it, and what has to be true. The documents stay with DoorLoop; this page lists what they covered.

## Operating model

- **Support plan for a new quarter**: hours, squads, QA, 1:1s and a leaderboard as one system. The apps in projects 3–5 came out of it.
- **Onboarding and Implementation after a merge**: the specialist blueprint, a 120-day handoff to Customer Success, and an onboarding-to-success flow.
- **A meeting cadence between CX and Product**: two separate forums merged into one monthly review, split by customer tenure.
- **Professional services launch plan**: the plan for launching a paid implementation offer.
- **Intake for CX Ops requests**: one front door with priorities, instead of DMs.
- **Platform migration off a CS tool**: a punch list of every workflow that depended on it and where each moved.

## Executive reviews

- **A CX business review with the CEO**, with a run-of-show.
- **Decision drivers for quarterly reviews**: each open question with a proposed answer and the case against.
- **A one-year plan for GTM operations**: two moves, sequenced, with a shared data layer underneath.

## Data foundations

- **A GTM data contract and catalog**: which system owns which field, definitions, and who can change them.
- **Release content tracker**: product releases mapped to the customer-facing content each one needs.

## Risk

- **Redesigned risk review for payments onboarding**: split one combined call into separate checks, with a decision engine and an agent that prepares evidence for the reviewer.

## Market and enablement

- **Competitive AI map**: how competitors' AI features compared, built with evidence tiers (shipped, announced, claimed) and a corrections log. Prepared for an industry conference.
- **Post-sale talk tracks** for CSMs and onboarding.
- **Spanish localisation** of customer documentation for Latin America, with a reusable product glossary.

## Decisions worth explaining

- **Foundations first, then optimisation.** Visibility and clear rules come before automation.
- **One owner, written down.** If a decision is made in a DM, it's effectively invisible.
- **Watch the incentives.** Every process change was checked against how it affected reps' compensation and targets.
